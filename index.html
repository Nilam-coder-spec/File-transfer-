About FileConnect

FileConnect is a simple and user-friendly platform designed to make file sharing between devices fast and convenient. It allows users to connect smartphones, tablets, laptops and computers using a unique 6-digit code. With an easy-to-use interface and a straightforward connection process, users can transfer files without complicated setup. Our goal is to make sharing files across devices easier and more accessible for everyone.
